Case study

Retrieval you can measure, not just demo.

Job Pipeline RAG Q&A over a personal document set

A retrieval-augmented Q&A system built on FastAPI. Embeddings come from a local sentence-transformer model, and the cosine-similarity retrieval index is written from scratch.

It's a hybrid design: exact and numeric questions skip the embeddings and go straight to structured data parsing, because vectors are bad at "how many" questions.

A rerunnable eval harness scores every change. Two rounds of debugging took answer accuracy from 20% to 100%.
